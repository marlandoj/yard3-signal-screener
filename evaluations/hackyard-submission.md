## Writeup (500 characters max)

TapeScope is a one-screen, explainable stock screener for a focused watchlist. It combines momentum, 52-week proximity, volume, trend, RSI, and valuation into a transparent score, then lets you inspect the evidence behind any signal. Filters, search, sorting, mobile layout, and a read-only prototype keep the decision loop fast without pretending to place trades.
